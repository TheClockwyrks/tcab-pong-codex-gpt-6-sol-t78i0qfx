# Carom

Two paddles, one luminous ball, and a field that never offers a straight answer. Carom is a fast local paddle duel where every return can change the angle of the rally.

Start in Solo against an opponent that tracks incoming shots, or choose Versus and share the keyboard. A point begins with a short serve countdown, then the ball cuts through the middle of the field. Reach 11 with a two point lead to take the match.

The trick is in your paddle's motion. Slide as you strike and the ball picks up spin, arcing toward a gap that looked closed a moment earlier. Hold still for a clean, direct return.

Two gold obstacles break up the crossing lanes. Thread between them, bank off a face, or send a curved shot past the paddle that thought it had the line covered.
